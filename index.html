Local-First Architecture

All your diary entries, moods, notes, and media are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted SQLCipher database. No servers, no cloud sync, no third-party access.

  • SQLCipher 256-bit encryption
  • Works completely offline
  • Instant sync-free writing
  • No subscription required

Military-Grade Security

Your content is protected with the same encryption technology used by governments and financial institutions, combined with biometric authentication for secure access.

FAQ

Quick answers that reduce user hesitation.

Does Dear Diary require an account?
No. The app is designed to work offline-first without requiring a cloud account.
Where is my data stored?
On your device. Content is intended to remain local, with optional app lock and on-device encryption support.
Can I export my mood history?
Yes. You can export reports in PDF / CSV / JSON formats that you control.
How do I contact support?
Email us anytime at mydeardiaryjournal@gmail.com or use the Support page.
