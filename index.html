FAQ

Quick answers that reduce user hesitation.

Can I use Dear Diary without internet?
Yes, 100% offline. Dear Diary is designed to work with no internet connection, always. It requires no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no server connection of any kind.
Does Dear Diary read my diary?
No. All intelligence — mood insights, trend analysis, keyword detection — runs locally on your device. No AI API is called. No data ever leaves your phone.
What happens if I forget my password?
Only your 12-word recovery phrase can restore access. The developer cannot help recover your data because we never had access to it. This is by design — you set up a recovery phrase during onboarding, and that is the only key.
Is my backup safe?
Yes. Your backup is protected by a password only you know — without it, the file is unreadable by anyone. It is a Premium feature.
What is Premium?
Premium unlocks unlimited Story Mode journeys, unlimited Daily Glimpse photos, automatic encrypted backup & restore, premium narration voices, and unlimited video exports.
Is Dear Diary available on iPhone?
No. Dear Diary is an Android-only app, available on the Google Play Store.
How do I contact support?
Email us anytime at support@mydeardiaryapp.com or visit the Support page.
