FAQ

Quick answers that reduce user hesitation.

Does Dear Diary require an account?
No. The app is designed to work offline-first without requiring a cloud account.
Where is my data stored?
On your device. Content is intended to remain local, with optional app lock and on-device encryption support.
Can I export my mood history?
Yes. You can export reports in PDF / CSV / JSON formats that you control.
How do I contact support?
Email us anytime at mydeardiaryjournal@gmail.com or use the Support page.
